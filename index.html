Frequently Asked Questions

Find your answers here.

+ What does "A-Book" actually mean?
A-Book means funded orders are passed through to external liquidity providers rather than filled internally. Your profit comes from the market hedge settling — not from our pocket. B-Book firms do the opposite: they keep positions in-house and profit when you lose.
+ How do I become an EZE Trader?
Choose a challenge size, complete the Trading Objectives (profit target within the loss limits), and pass verification. Your EZE Account with A-Book routing is issued the same day.
+ How fast are payouts, really?
Median across all methods in the last 30 days: 3 minutes 40 seconds. E-wallets clear in about 90 seconds; SEPA Instant in a few minutes; SWIFT takes as long as the receiving bank takes. There is no payout calendar and no minimum amount.
+ Can I trade news?
Yes. NFP, CPI, rate decisions — all allowed, on evaluation and funded accounts alike. No blackout windows.
+ Do I have to close positions overnight or before the weekend?
No. Overnight and weekend holding are both allowed. Swap-free account options are available on request.
+ I have successfully passed, what do I do now?
Complete KYC (one time, ~5 minutes), verify a payout method, and your EZE Account credentials arrive by email — usually the same day. Your first withdrawal also refunds your challenge fee.
+ Can I modify my platform or account type?
Yes, before your first trade on the account. Contact support via live chat and they'll reissue credentials on your preferred platform.
+ Is the evaluation also on A-Book?
No — evaluations run in a simulated environment on live pricing, which is what keeps challenge fees affordable. Funded accounts are live A-Book. We state this openly because most firms never route you to the market at all.
