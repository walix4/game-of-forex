What does "A-Book" actually mean?

A-Book means your orders are passed through to external liquidity providers — banks and ECN venues — rather than being filled internally by the firm. Your profit comes from the market hedge settling, not from the firm's pocket.

B-Book firms do the opposite: they keep your positions in-house, which means your loss is their revenue. That conflict of interest quietly shapes everything — tighter rules, slower payouts, more reasons to fail you.

Every funded EZE Account runs 100% A-Book. Read the full breakdown, including a live order trace, on the A-Book Technology page.

How are the loss limits measured?

Two limits protect every account:

  • Max daily loss — resets at server midnight, measured against that day's starting balance or equity, whichever is higher.
  • Max overall loss — static from your starting balance. It never trails your profits: make 8% and you still have the full original buffer below the start.

Your dashboard shows live distance to both limits at all times, so a breach never comes as a surprise.
