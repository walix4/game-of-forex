General

Platforms, A-Book, KYC & countries
+ What does "A-Book" actually mean?
A-Book means funded orders are passed through to external liquidity providers rather than filled internally. Your profit comes from the market hedge settling — not from our pocket. B-Book firms do the opposite: they keep positions in-house and profit when you lose.
+ How do I become an EZE Trader?
Choose a challenge size, complete the Trading Objectives (profit target within the loss limits), and pass verification. Your EZE Account with A-Book routing is issued the same day.
+ What's the difference between 2-Step, 1-Step and Instant?
2-Step has two phases (10% then 5% targets) and the lowest fees. 1-Step has a single 10% target. Instant skips evaluation entirely — you start funded with tighter loss limits and an up-to-90% split. All three end on the same live A-Book account.
+ Is there a time limit or minimum trading days?
No and no. Take a week or take a year — the account stays open as long as you respect the loss limits. There are no minimum trading days on any route.
+ I have successfully passed, what do I do now?
Complete KYC (one time, ~5 minutes), verify a payout method, and your EZE Account credentials arrive by email — usually the same day. Your first withdrawal also refunds your challenge fee.
+ What happens if I fail the evaluation?
The account closes and you can start a fresh challenge whenever you're ready. Failing an evaluation never carries a penalty beyond the fee, and retake discounts appear in your dashboard regularly.
+ How fast are payouts, really?
Median across all methods in the last 30 days: 3 minutes 40 seconds. E-wallets clear in about 90 seconds; SEPA Instant in a few minutes; SWIFT takes as long as the receiving bank takes. There is no payout calendar and no minimum amount.
+ What's the profit split and how does it grow?
Funded accounts start at 80% and climb to 95% as you hit scaling milestones. Instant Funding accounts run up to 90%. Once you reach a tier, it never goes back down.
+ When is my challenge fee refunded?
On evaluation routes (2-Step and 1-Step), the one-time fee is returned in full with your first payout from the funded account. Instant Funding fees aren't refundable — that route skips evaluation entirely.
+ Are there payout fees or minimums?
No fees on any rail — the amount you request is the amount that arrives — and no minimum withdrawal. Request $50 or $50,000, as often as you like.
+ Can I trade news?
Yes. NFP, CPI, rate decisions — all allowed, on evaluation and funded accounts alike. No blackout windows.
+ Do I have to close positions overnight or before the weekend?
No. Overnight and weekend holding are both allowed. Swap-free account options are available on request.
+ How are the loss limits measured?
Max daily loss resets at server midnight and is measured against that day's starting balance or equity, whichever is higher. Max overall loss is static from your starting balance — it never trails your profits.
+ Are expert advisors and algos allowed?
Yes — your own EAs and strategies are welcome. What's forbidden is exploiting the environment itself: latency arbitrage, feed manipulation and group copy-exploits end the account.
+ What instruments can I trade?
Forex majors, minors and crosses, gold and metals, major indices and popular crypto pairs — the same list on evaluation and funded accounts. The full symbol list lives in your dashboard.
+ Which platforms are supported?
MT5, cTrader, DXtrade and Match-Trader. You pick at checkout, and support can reissue credentials on another platform before your first trade on the account.
+ Can I modify my platform or account type?
Yes, before your first trade on the account. Contact support via live chat and they'll reissue credentials on your preferred platform.
+ Is the evaluation also on A-Book?
No — evaluations run in a simulated environment on live pricing, which is what keeps challenge fees affordable. Funded accounts are live A-Book. We state this openly because most firms never route you to the market at all.
+ Which countries do you support?
Traders in 132 countries use EZE today. A short list of sanctioned jurisdictions is excluded — the checkout will tell you immediately if yours is affected.
