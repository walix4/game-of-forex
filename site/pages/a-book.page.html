The evaluation runs in a realistic simulated environment so we can assess your risk. Once you're funded, your account trades on live A-Book routing to real liquidity.
How does EZE make money then?+
From trading volume and commission passed through to liquidity providers, plus challenge fees — never from your trading losses.
Does A-Book mean slower fills?+
No. Orders are aggregated across tier-1 venues and filled in milliseconds at raw interbank prices, with no dealing-desk intervention.
Can I verify my orders reached the market?+
Every funded account's execution report includes venue timestamps and fill IDs. Ask support for your routing log any time — it's your data.
